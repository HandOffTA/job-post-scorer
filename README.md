# Job Post Scorer

> Part of the [HandOffTA](https://github.com/HandOffTA) portfolio: practical AI tools for hiring teams.

## What It Does

Rates a job posting for readability, jargon, and exclusionary language, then suggests specific fixes.

## The Problem It Solves

Many job posts are long, full of internal jargon, or use language that quietly discourages qualified people from applying. That shrinks your applicant pool before you've even started.

## Who It's For

Recruiters writing or reviewing job posts, hiring managers drafting JDs, and employer brand or DEI teams auditing postings.

## How It Works

1. **Paste the job post.** Full text, as it would appear on your careers page.
2. **Score it.** The post is rated on readability, jargon density, and potentially exclusionary language.
3. **Highlight issues.** Problem phrases are called out with a short explanation of why they matter.
4. **Suggest fixes.** You get rewrite suggestions you can accept as-is or adjust.
